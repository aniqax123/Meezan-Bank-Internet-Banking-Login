How to Prevent Phishing Attacks

A Security Pillar Guide by Meezan Bank

Your Digital Safety is Our Priority

Meezan Bank is committed to providing the safest digital banking experience. As a pioneer in Islamic banking, we implement rigorous multi-layer security protocols to protect our customers. However, the biggest defense against online fraud is an informed and aware customer.

This guide will educate you on the various phishing techniques scammers use, how to identify them, and how to manage Meezan Bank's specific security features—especially the Security Image—to keep your funds safe.

Common Types of Phishing Attacks

Scammers use several methods to trick you into revealing sensitive information. Meezan Bank categorizes these into four main attack vectors:

📱 Smishing (SMS)

Fraudulent text messages pretending to be from Meezan Bank. These often contain urgent warnings (e.g., "Account locked") and a malicious link. Meezan Bank never sends clickable security links via SMS.

📞 Vishing (Voice)

Phone calls from scammers impersonating Meezan Bank support agents. They claim to be "verifying" your identity and ask for your PIN or OTP. Meezan Bank will never ask for your full banking PIN over the phone.

📧 Email Phishing

Official-looking emails with fake logos and fake sender addresses. They urge you to click a link to "update your banking profile." Always check the sender's email address. Meezan Bank emails come from @meezanbank.com.

🌐 Pharming & Clone Sites

Hackers create a website that looks exactly like the real Meezan Bank Internet Banking portal. You type in your ID and password, and they steal them instantly. This is why Meezan Bank uses a Security Image!

Understanding Meezan Bank's Security Image

When you log in to Meezan Bank Internet Banking, you must select a Security Image from a grid of icons (like a clock, bicycle, or plane) and a corresponding label.

💡 Why does Meezan Bank use this?
It is designed to prevent Pharming. When you set up your Security Image, Meezan Bank stores your selection on its official secure servers. Every time you log in, the system shows you your chosen image.

If you land on a fake, malicious website: The fake site does not have access to Meezan Bank's database, so it cannot show you your specific image. If the image grid doesn't match your chosen one, close the page immediately—you are on a phishing site!

How to Forgot / Reset Your Security Image

If you have forgotten which image you selected, or if you feel your previous security image may have been compromised, Meezan Bank makes it simple to reset it securely. Important: You must do this on the official website, never through a link sent via SMS or email.

Follow these exact steps to reset your Security Image:

1
Go to the Official Portal: Open your browser and manually type https://ebanking.meezanbank.com. Never click links from emails.
2
Click "Forgot Security Image?": On the main login screen, enter your Username (or ID). Just below the Security Image grid, look for the text link "Forgot Security Image?".
3
Verify Your Identity: The system will immediately prompt you to verify your identity. This is a mandatory security measure to ensure no one else can change your security image.
4
Enter the OTP: Meezan Bank will send a one-time 8-digit PIN (OTP) to your registered mobile number. Enter this OTP into the provided field.
5
Select a New Image: Once verified, you will be taken to a fresh grid of security images. Choose a new image and its corresponding label that is easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess. Click submit, and your security will be instantly updated.
🚨 Reminder for your project: Remember—Scammers will often try to trick you into clicking a link that says "Reset Security Image." Meezan Bank will never send this link to you. You must always navigate to the official portal yourself.

5 Golden Rules to Prevent Phishing

  • Verify the Source: Meezan Bank will never ask for your full password, PIN, or card numbers via phone, SMS, or email.
  • Check the URL: Always check the URL address bar. Legitimate Meezan Bank sites always contain "meezanbank.com". If it uses hyphens or "secure-" prefixes, it is a scam.
  • Don't Trust Urgency: Phishers try to panic you. Take a deep breath. If an SMS says "Your account will be blocked in 15 minutes," call the official Meezan Bank helpline (listed on your debit card) to verify, rather than clicking the link.
  • Hover Before You Click: On a desktop, hover your mouse over links in emails. The real destination URL will pop up at the bottom of your screen. If it doesn't match the text, it's a phishing attempt.
  • Use the Official App: Download the official "Meezan Bank" mobile app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. The app provides the most secure way to verify transactions without using mobile browsers.

What to Do If You Suspect a Phishing Attempt

If you believe you have clicked a malicious link or entered your credentials into a fake Meezan Bank page:

  1. Immediately change your password via the official Meezan Bank Internet Banking portal.
  2. Immediately contact Meezan Bank's 24/7 helpline at 111-331-331 (or +92-21-111-331-331) to block your account temporarily.
  3. Reset your Security Image by following Step 1-5 above.
  4. Report the phishing attempt to Meezan Bank's security department via security@meezanbank.com so they can block the malicious site.
